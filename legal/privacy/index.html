Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by you when submitting forms on our website (including "Book a Demo" and "Request a Quote"). This includes your full name, work email address, company name, job role, and any details provided regarding your organisation's financial infrastructure.

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected via our website forms is routed directly to saakshi@vinayakafinancials.com for the sole purpose of scheduling product walkthroughs, preparing tailored pricing quotes, and responding to direct inquiries. We do not sell, pool, or share submitted information with third parties.

3. Data Security & Sovereignty

Vinayaka Financials enforces strict operational security standards. Any operational client data processed within SAVI platforms remains governed under enterprise contracts and client-controlled infrastructure parameters.

4. Retention & Deletion Rights

You have the right to request deletion or modification of your contact records at any time by emailing saakshi@vinayakafinancials.com.
