Intelligence on top of the systems you already run.

Replacing a finance system is a decision most organisations postpone for years, and they are right to be careful. The Intelligence Platform removes that decision from the equation. Your ERP stays exactly where it is. SAVI reads from it and gives your team answers on top.

How it fits

System of Record Preserved

Your existing ERP remains the system of record. SAVI does not take over your ledger.

Traceable Answer Layer

SAVI reads your financial data and builds a traceable answer layer above it.

Direct Record Drill-Down

Every answer opens into the records it came from, inside your own system.

Zero Workflow Disruption

Your team keeps working the way they already work.

What you get

Ask questions about your finances in plain English and get answers computed from your own data.

Full source tracing on every figure, down to the originating record.

Multi-entity and multi-jurisdiction rule resolution across your group.

Honest gap reporting, SAVI states what it cannot answer rather than estimating.

Supported systems

SAVI is designed to work alongside the major finance systems in use today, including SAP, Oracle, Tally, Zoho, and QuickBooks, as well as data exported directly from your existing reporting.
