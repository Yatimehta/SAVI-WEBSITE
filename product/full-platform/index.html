Your entire finance function, on one system.

For organisations ready to consolidate, SAVI runs the whole finance operation, the ledger your team works in every day, and the intelligence layer that answers questions about it. One system, one source of truth, and the same traceability promise applied to every figure it produces.

What's included

Core accounting

  • Double-entry ledger with balance enforced at the point of entry, not corrected afterwards
  • Flexible chart of accounts, with structured import
  • Validated data import that rejects unbalanced files rather than partially loading them
  • Multi-tier approval workflows with role-based permissions

Reporting and compliance

  • Financial reporting with full drill-down to source records
  • Indian GST reporting, including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, e-invoicing, IMS, and reconciliation
  • Board-ready report packs

Oversight

  • Fraud and anomaly detection across transactions, duplicate payments, split invoices, and irregular vendor patterns
  • Immutable audit trail recording every change, with before and after values

Intelligence

  • Everything included in the Intelligence Platform, applied to your SAVI ledger directly
