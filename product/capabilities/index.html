What SAVI does.

Grouped by the work it supports. Every capability listed here operates on your own data and reports figures traceable to their source.

Ask and analyse

  • Plain-English questions answered from your own financial records
  • Full trace from any figure to the records behind it
  • Drill-down through reports to underlying transactions

Accounting and control

  • Double-entry ledger with enforced balance at entry
  • Chart of accounts management and import
  • Multi-tier, role-gated approval workflows
  • Immutable audit trail with before and after values on every change

Risk and assurance

  • Statistical fraud detection powered by Benford's Law leading-digit analysis
  • Anomaly pattern detection: duplicate payments, split invoices, round numbers, weekend spikes
  • Continuous risk scoring across millions of transactions with automated audit actions
  • Honest gap reporting: SAVI explicitly states what cannot be determined instead of estimating

Compliance

  • Indian GST: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, e-invoicing, IMS, reconciliation
  • Multi-jurisdiction rule resolution across incorporation, operations, ownership, and reporting standard

Group and cross-border

  • Multi-entity structures with ownership relationships
  • Per-entity rule resolution with each rule traced to the fact that triggered it
