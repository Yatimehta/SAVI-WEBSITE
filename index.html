The finance function has an accuracy problem. AI made it worse.

Most finance tools operate on approximations. When asked a question, general-purpose AI models produce fluent answers, but in financial reporting, a fluent wrong number is more dangerous than an obvious error. It gets believed, reported, and acted upon.

Finance teams end up spending more time validating numbers than using them. SAVI was built to eliminate that gap.

Computed from your data. Explained by AI. Traced to the source.

Calculated, not generated

Every figure SAVI reports is computed deterministically from your underlying financial records. The AI explains the result, it never invents the number.

Traceable to the row

Click any figure in any answer or report to see the formula, the query, the filtered rows, and the originating transaction inside your system of record.

Honest about gaps

When underlying data is missing or incomplete, SAVI states the gap clearly instead of estimating. An explicit unknown is safer than a confident guess.

Multiple entities. Multiple jurisdictions. One clear picture.

Most finance tools decide which rules apply to a company by looking at a single country field. Real organisations are not that simple. A company incorporated in one country, operating in three others, owned by a parent in a fifth, and reporting under a sixth standard has four different sets of obligations at once, and getting that wrong is where cross-border finance breaks down.

SAVI resolves the rules for every entity from four dimensions at once: where it is incorporated, where it operates, who owns it, and who it reports to. Each resolved rule traces back to the specific fact that triggered it.
