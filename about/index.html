Why we built SAVI.

Finance teams are surrounded by systems that produce numbers and short of systems that can prove them. The gap has widened as AI has entered the category: tools that generate financial figures can generate incorrect ones just as convincingly, and in a discipline where every reported number may have to be defended, that is not a small flaw.

SAVI was built around the opposite principle. The numbers are computed from your own records and carry their lineage with them. The AI explains what was computed; it never produces the figure itself. Where the data does not support an answer, SAVI says so.

It is a deliberately conservative design for a category that has moved quickly. We think finance is exactly where that conservatism belongs.

What we're building toward

A finance function where no reported figure has to be taken on faith — where anyone, at any level, can open a number and see precisely what it is made of.
